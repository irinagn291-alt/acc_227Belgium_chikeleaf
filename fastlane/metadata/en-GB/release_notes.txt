Version 1.0. First release of Hingeleaf, a personal stamp album for people who file stamp wants and owned copies on one leaf.

• Onboarding under HINGELEAF: Seat the next stamp, Cancel the hinge, Grade only what you own. Skip or Start filing opens THE LEAF.
• THE LEAF with pages of 12 perforations, Previous leaf, Next leaf, Add a leaf and In the tray.
• DISCOVER search by country, topic or catalogue number. SCAN a retail pack barcode, QR code or typed Pack code or link. Saved album shelf when the catalogue has no match or does not answer.
• Hinge a loose stamp as a want. Wants lists hinged stamps until they are cancelled.
• Cancel a hinge when the paper is in hand (In hand.). Cancel without a hinge counts as skew on Taste; the stamp stays in the tray. Clear from tray.
• Grade 1 to 5 only after cancel. Local note on a graded cancelled copy.
• Taste from Profile: country grade totals, average grade, eras, notes, skew and drift. Crate of owned cancelled copies. Seen cancel ledger by day.
• Peel last mark. Export album folio then Share album folio. Haptics. Run onboarding again. Erase everything. Contact Hingeleaf. Catalogue credit, Colnect.

The leaf starts blank. Unfinished work resumes on the next launch.
