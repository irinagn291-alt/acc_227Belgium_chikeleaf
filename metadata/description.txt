Hingeleaf is a personal stamp album for people who file stamp wants and owned copies on one leaf. Search or scan a catalog issue, seat it on a perforation as a want, cancel that hinge when the paper stamp is in hand, then grade the owned copy from 1 to 5. Taste totals those cancelled grades by country.

• THE LEAF shows pages of 12 perforations, with Previous leaf, Next leaf, and Add a leaf.
• An empty spot shows Open. Tap it to open DISCOVER.
• DISCOVER uses the field Country, topic, or number. Search stays dim until the field has text.
• Scan reads a retail pack barcode or QR code, or you type Pack code or link. Look up stays dim until that field has text.
• If the catalog has no match or does not answer, Show the shelf fills Discover with the saved album shelf.
• A successful Search drops matching issues into In the tray as loose stamps.
• Hinge seats a selected tray stamp on an empty perforation as a want. Wants lists hinged stamps until you cancel.
• Cancel marks a hinged want in hand. In hand. appears briefly. Cancel without a hinge records a skew and leaves the stamp loose. Clear from tray removes a loose stamp.
• Grade capsules 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5, and the field Local note, appear only after cancel.
• Profile opens TASTE, which counts cancelled grades only: Grade total by country, Average grade, Skew, Drift, Eras, and Notes.
• CRATE lists owned cancelled copies. SEEN lists cancel days, newest day last.
• Peel last mark asks Peel the last mark? and removes the latest hinge or cancel.
• SETTINGS includes Haptics, Run onboarding again, Export album folio then Share album folio, Erase everything, Contact Hingeleaf, and Catalog credit, Colnect.

Hinges, cancels, grades, and notes stay on this device. English UI, portrait, iPhone and iPad, iOS 17.0 or later.
